# Supply Chain Assessment - Home-Based Business Proposals

## Review basis

This is a provisional desk review of the 16 submitted proposal PDFs. Scores assess the quality and feasibility of each proposal from a supply-chain and procurement perspective; they are not a final eligibility or protection decision.

### Scoring rubric (100 points converted to 1-10)

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Asset need and technical fit | 25% |
| Sourcing evidence and price realism | 20% |
| Budget coherence and applicant contribution | 15% |
| Operating inputs and supply continuity | 15% |
| Implementation capacity and commercial feasibility | 15% |
| Risk, compliance, maintenance and sustainability | 10% |

Decision guide: **8-10 = prioritize**, **6-7 = consider with conditions**, **1-5 = revise or do not prioritize**.

## Cross-cutting procurement findings

1. **Prices are not yet procurement-ready.** Retail links and applicant estimates are useful market references, but award files should contain at least two or three comparable, current quotations for material items, inclusive of VAT, delivery, installation, training and warranty.
2. **Several totals need reconciliation.** The DRC request, personal contribution, asset-list sum and total investment do not always align clearly. The finance tables also contain apparent missing zeros or data-entry errors.
3. **Working capital is underdeveloped.** Many plans focus on equipment but give limited evidence for the first three months of consumables, utilities, repairs, packaging and inventory replenishment.
4. **After-sales service matters.** High-value machines should be sourced from vendors able to provide local installation, warranty service and spare parts; marketplace availability alone is not enough.
5. **Home-based eligibility must be checked separately.** Car washes, salons, food/coffee operations and some machinery may require commercial premises, zoning, hygiene, environmental or occupational-safety approval.
6. **Cash forecasts need stress testing.** Recalculate monthly profit at 60%, 80% and 100% of projected sales, including owner labor, taxes, maintenance and replacement reserves.

## Minimum conditions before purchase order or grant award

- Reconciled and signed bill of quantities with essential/optional classification.
- Comparable quotations and documented bid analysis for high-value assets.
- Vendor identity, tax status, delivery lead time, warranty and service location.
- Site verification covering space, power, water, drainage, ventilation and security.
- Licenses/certifications for food, cosmetics, beauty devices, electrical work or environmental discharge as applicable.
- Three-month working-capital plan and supplier list for recurring inputs.
- Conflict-of-interest and sanctions screening under DRC procedures.
- Asset tagging, handover, user training, maintenance plan and post-distribution monitoring.

## Important limitation

The grades reflect the content and internal consistency of the submitted PDFs only. Prices, vendors, legal eligibility, applicant identity, premises, market demand and technical claims were not independently verified. Final selection should combine this score with program eligibility, vulnerability/protection criteria, field verification and DRC procurement controls.
